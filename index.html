Modern Teaching Philosophies

Author: Hazbleidy García · Format: Training handbook / educational resource · Year: 2025

This professional training resource was created for inlingua Terni and designed to equip language tutors and trainers with practical skills, reflective strategies, and modern methodological tools for learner-centred, communicative teaching.

The material combines theory with hands-on practice through observation, peer collaboration, and scenario-based decision-making. It draws on active learning, inlingua DICE methodology, and the Agile Learning Cycle to help participants apply classroom principles in authentic teaching contexts.

Objectives

  • Apply a learner-centred approach by placing learners at the centre of instruction.
  • Identify and analyse learner needs through observation, diagnostics, and short interviews.
  • Use active learning strategies to promote problem-solving, interaction, and higher-order thinking.
  • Deliver structured, communicative lessons with clear instructions, ICQs, CCQs, scaffolding, and feedback.
  • Reflect on teaching practice and demonstrate competence through micro-teaching.
  • Promote inclusive, sustainable, and quality education aligned with international teaching standards.

Core content

  • Learner-centred approach and active learning philosophy
  • Learner needs analysis and diagnosis
  • Step-by-step lesson delivery frameworks
  • Classroom communication: instructions, checking understanding, error correction
  • Mistakes versus errors: identification and response
  • Teacher roles and professional responsibilities
  • Modern teaching approaches and lesson planning
  • inlingua DICE methodology
  • Micro-teaching practice and reflection

Little Learners of English as a Foreign Language

Author: Hazbleidy García · Format: Interactive learning programme · Target audience: Students aged 8–12

An interactive, communicative learning programme designed specifically for young learners preparing for Cambridge English exams. The programme combines structured exam-preparation content with engaging, age-appropriate activities that develop oral communication skills through role play, storytelling, and authentic communicative tasks.

Purpose

  • Develop English communication skills aligned with Cambridge exam requirements.
  • Build confidence and fluency in spoken English through role play and interactive storytelling.
  • Provide guided pathways that prepare learners for Cambridge oral skill assessments.
  • Engage young learners through narrative-driven activities and collaborative communication tasks.

Key features

  • Interactive content: Role plays, dialogue practice, and story-based activities that mirror real-world communication.
  • Cambridge exam alignment: Activities structured to develop the speaking and listening skills assessed in Cambridge Young Learners exams (Starters, Movers, Flyers).
  • Oral skill development: Guided speaking tasks with scaffolding, prompts, and peer-interaction activities.
  • Storytelling approach: Narrative-driven learning paths that engage children and provide context for language practice.
  • Age-appropriate design: Visual, interactive, and communicative materials suited to 8–12-year-old learners.

Content areas

  • Communicative role plays and dialogue exchanges
  • Interactive stories with speaking opportunities
  • Guided oral practice aligned to Cambridge exam skills
  • Vocabulary and grammar in communicative contexts
  • Listening comprehension through narratives and authentic audio
  • Confidence-building through repeated, scaffolded speaking practice
