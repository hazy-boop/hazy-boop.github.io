About Me

I am an instructional designer and learning content developer with 13 years of experience in education and training. I founded and built a language centre in Colombia, accredited by the Ministry of Education, and since 2016 I have designed learning content, coordinated trainers, and translated complex ideas into clear, engaging materials for learners.

I plan projects around clear objectives, coordinate timelines and approvals across teams, and deliver complete learning resources such as manuals, interactive activities, and visual materials. I also configure and train custom AI assistants to support content production efficiently and scale those tools across teams.

My strengths are organisation, clarity, bilingual communication, and the ability to turn complex topics into approachable, learner-ready experiences.
